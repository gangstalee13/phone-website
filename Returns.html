Returns & Refunds

Our local warranty and return process for devices bought from iPhone Shop.

Local warranty

Every device is backed by our local warranty, handled by our team in Masvingo. If your iPhone develops a fault under normal use, contact us by phone or WhatsApp to arrange an inspection.

Dead-on-arrival or wrong item

If your device doesn't power on out of the box, or you received the wrong model, contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We'll arrange a replacement or full refund once the issue is confirmed.

Change of mind

For pre-order deposits, contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel — deposits on items not yet shipped can typically be refunded, minus any non-recoverable import costs already incurred.

What isn't covered

Physical damage, water damage, or unauthorized repairs after delivery are not covered under the local warranty.

How to start a claim

Message us on WhatsApp with your order reference and a description of the issue, and we'll guide you through the next steps.
