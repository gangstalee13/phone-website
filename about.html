About iPhone Shop

We curate the latest iPhone models and help you explore features with immersive visuals.

Our Brand

From the United States to Your Doorstep in Zimbabwe

We founded our business to give Zimbabwean consumers access to premium, authentic mobile technology without the inflated local price tags or the risks of buying unverified used devices.

Clean Devices, Verified Supply

Every iPhone is sourced directly from major carrier auctions and certified tech suppliers in the United States, then tested before it leaves the US to ensure clean IMEI and never-locally-repaired condition.

Secure Logistics Pipeline

Devices are air-freighted to Zimbabwe, cleared through customs, and processed at our Masvingo headquarters for final inspection, packaging, and dispatch.

Nationwide Reach

Operating from Masvingo, we deliver to Harare, Bulawayo, Gweru, Mutare, Victoria Falls, and beyond through trusted local courier partners.

Our Realistic Goals for 2027 & Beyond

48-Hour Nationwide Delivery

Our central Masvingo location helps optimize transit routes so stock items can reach customers in under 48 hours.

Transparent Pre-Order Fulfillment

Custom model or out-of-stock orders are streamlined through air-freight for delivery in 5 to 7 days.

Local Collection Points

We plan secure collection and drop-off depots in Harare and Bulawayo for warranty visits, pickups, and trade-ins.

Eco-Friendly Packaging

We aim to move to biodegradable locally manufactured packaging within the next year, reducing our footprint and supporting Zimbabwean printers.

🛡️ The Local Warranty Promise

Although your device comes from the US, your warranty and support are handled locally by our team in Masvingo. If you need help, we are available by phone or WhatsApp.

Thank you for supporting a growing Zimbabwean business and trusting us with your next upgrade.
