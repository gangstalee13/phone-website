Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. This explains what information iPhone Shop collects and how it's used.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect the details you provide on the checkout form: your name, email address, and payment reference details (such as an Ecocash phone number or bank account name, for verifying your own payment to us). We do not collect card numbers or banking passwords — payments are made directly through Ecocash or your bank, not through this website.

Identity verification (KYC)

To help prevent fraud, every order requires a national ID or passport number and a photo of that document. This is stored securely on our server, only accessible to our team, and used solely to confirm your identity for the order. We don't share it with third parties except where required by law.

Cart data

Items you add to your cart are stored locally in your own browser (localStorage) so your selections are remembered between visits. This data stays on your device and is not automatically sent to us until you submit an order.

How we use your information

Order details are used only to process and deliver your purchase, confirm payment, and contact you about your order. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Contacting us

If you reach out by WhatsApp or email, we keep that conversation to respond to your enquiry and provide support.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy or want your information removed from our records, contact us through the Contact page.
